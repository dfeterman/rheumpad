Your library

Everything you edit here saves in this browser automatically. Use Export (top right) to back your library up to a file, and Import to load it on another computer. Do this whenever you make big edits: clinic workstations sometimes wipe browser data.

Blank syntax for phrase and template bodies: {dose} makes a fill-in box, {severity:mild|moderate|severe} makes a dropdown, *** makes a generic blank, same as Epic. Put a ~ in front of the first option, as in {dose:~1 mg daily|2 mg daily}, and the list becomes a typable box that suggests those values and accepts anything else.

Your signature

Type your signature once. Every note, letter, and attestation that ends in a signature uses it, including the modular follow-up and consult notes and all prior authorization letters. Leave it blank and notes print [your name and credentials] instead.

Put [[ME]] anywhere in your own phrases or templates to drop the whole block in, or [[MENAME]] for the first line alone inside a sentence. Your signature saves with your library, so an Export you hand to a colleague carries your name. Tell them to change it in this box.

EMR mode

Tailored templates carry portable markers like [[NAME]], [[MEDS]], and [[LAB:ESR]] for the parts your chart fills in by itself. This switch decides what those markers become when you copy a note. Flip it any time; the same template works in both.

Generic mode writes cues as plain text, never as boxes you type into. No name, date of birth, or MRN is ever entered into RheumPad in either mode.

What RheumPad is

A charting companion for rheumatology. It holds your dot phrases, note templates, pick lists, patient handouts, drug cards, calculators, a steroid taper builder, peri-op and pregnancy references, and an E/M helper. You fill in blanks here, copy, and paste into whatever chart you use. It works in Epic, Meditech, Cerner, Athena, or on paper.

It runs entirely inside your browser. No login, no server, no internet connection needed after the file loads. Nothing you type leaves your computer.

Never type patient identifiers into RheumPad. No names, no dates of birth, no MRNs, not in the pre-chart list and not in phrases you write yourself. The app is built so identifiers are never needed: chart-specific details show as bracketed cues that your EMR fills in after you paste.

Set it up in three minutes

  1. Type your signature. Settings, Your signature. Every note, letter, and attestation picks it up.
  2. Type your clinic name. Settings, Your library. It prints on patient handouts.
  3. Pick your EMR. Settings, EMR mode. Epic turns markers into SmartLinks. Any other chart leaves them as bracketed cues you overwrite or delete.
  4. Export a backup. Top right. Do this again after any big editing session, because clinic computers wipe browser data without warning.

The three things worth learning first

1. The quick launcher. Press the period key or Ctrl+K from anywhere. Type a dot phrase like .mtxstart, or any name. Arrow keys move, Enter copies or opens it. Phrases with no blanks copy straight to your clipboard.

2. The modular notes. Note Templates, Modular Notes. Open Follow-Up Note or New Consult Note, pick a disease from the dropdown at the top, and the assessment, interval history, serologies, focused exam, and labs rewrite themselves for that disease. Pick nothing for a plain generic note. The SECTIONS chips underneath hide the parts you do not use, and hidden sections stay out of Copy full note.

3. Blanks. Anywhere you write your own text: {dose} makes a fill-in box, {severity:mild|moderate|severe} makes a dropdown, *** makes a plain blank. Boxes you leave empty copy as *** so you spot them in the note.

Make it yours

Settings edits every phrase, template, pick list, handout, and drug card, and adds new ones. Nothing is locked. The starter library is a starting point, so rewrite anything that does not match how you practice.

Star anything to pin it to the bar under the search box. Starred items are the ones you reach for ten times a day.

Moving to another computer: Export here, Import there. The file carries your whole library, including your signature, so change the signature after importing someone else's.

Before you rely on it

Drug dosing, monitoring intervals, taper schedules, peri-op holds, pregnancy compatibility, and calculator output are a reference starting point written by one rheumatologist. Verify against current prescribing information and your own guidelines before you act on any of it. RheumPad is a documentation aid. The clinical decision stays yours.
