Rheum billing pearls

DMARD and biologic therapy with lab monitoring is "drug therapy requiring intensive monitoring for toxicity": a HIGH risk element. Combined with a moderate or high problem level, many routine rheum follow-ups legitimately support 99214-99215 when documented.
Name each lab you reviewed, each image you personally interpreted, and each outside record: every one is a data point.
Deciding to CONTINUE a prescription is prescription drug management. Say it in the plan ("continue methotrexate 20 mg weekly after review of labs and disease activity").
Level needs 2 of 3 columns. Time counts everything on the calendar day: chart review, documentation, orders, portal messages. Bill on time or MDM, whichever lands higher.
This tool supports complete documentation of work you actually did; the level must always match the visit.
